Remote Work Policy

Version 1.0 — Effective date: February 1, 2024 — Owner: People Operations

1. Purpose

This policy sets out the conditions under which employees may work from locations other than a company office.

2. Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probationary period of six months are eligible to request remote work.

Roles that require on-site presence, such as facilities and lab staff, are not eligible.

Eligibility is reviewed by the employee's manager and confirmed by People Operations.

3. Work Arrangements

Hybrid employees must be in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

4. Working Hours and Availability

Remote employees must be available during core hours of 10:00 to 16:00 in their local time zone.

Employees must respond to messages from their team within two hours during core hours.

5. Equipment and Expenses

The company provides a laptop, monitor and headset to every remote employee.

Employees receive a one-time home office stipend of USD 300.

Internet costs are the responsibility of the employee.

6. Security

  • Use the company VPN when accessing internal systems.
  • Lock your screen whenever you step away from your device.
  • Do not work from public Wi-Fi networks.

7. Working from Abroad

Employees may work from outside their country of employment for up to 30 days per calendar year, with manager approval.

8. Review

This policy will be reviewed every two years by People Operations.
