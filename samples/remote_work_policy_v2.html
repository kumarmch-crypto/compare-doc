Remote Work Policy

Version 2.0 — Effective date: September 1, 2025 — Owner: People Operations

1. Purpose

This policy sets out the conditions under which employees may work from locations other than a company office.

2. Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probationary period of three months are eligible to request remote work.

Eligibility is reviewed by the employee's manager and confirmed by People Operations.

Roles that require on-site presence, such as facilities, lab and reception staff, are not eligible.

Hybrid employees must be in the office on their team's designated anchor days.

4. Working Hours and Availability

Remote employees must be available during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone.

Employees must respond to messages from their team within two hours during core hours.

5. Equipment and Expenses

The company provides a laptop, monitor and headset to every remote employee.

Employees receive a one-time home office stipend of USD 500.

The company reimburses internet costs of up to USD 50 per month for hybrid and fully remote employees.

6. Security

  • Use the company VPN when accessing internal systems.
  • Lock your screen whenever you step away from your device.
  • Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the company VPN enabled.
  • Report lost or stolen devices to IT Security within 24 hours.

7. Working from Abroad

Employees may work from outside their country of employment for up to 45 days per calendar year, with manager and People Operations approval.

Working from countries subject to export or sanctions restrictions is not permitted.

8. Health and Wellbeing

Remote employees are encouraged to take regular breaks and may request an ergonomic assessment of their home workspace once per year.

9. Review

This policy will be reviewed annually by People Operations.
